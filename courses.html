MATH THROUGH ART OFFERINGS:

K-5

One Off: Nancy can visit your classroom and teach a lesson on the topic of your choice. With each visit, Nancy will share a youtube video with the classroom teacher so they can teach the project independently in the future. Deeper Dive: Nancy can visit for a series of lessons and share sequences of projects for a more in depth exploration of a math topic/unit (ie place value, multiplication, fractions, geometry, measurement etc) Teaching videos for each project shared with classroom teacher.

Math Through Art Throughout the Year for 2nd and 3rd grades

Several schools have selected this offering for MTA to visit 2nd or 3rd grade classrooms once a month throughout the school year. This is like a sample platter of MTA projects. Students get to develop their MTA abilities over the course of an entire year and enjoy projects that cover a variety of topics. Teaching videos are also included for classroom teachers.

Teachers/Educators

Professional Development: Nancy can provide workshops for educators during in service days. Math Through Art also offers workshops at regional conferences for educators, Nancy has presented at VELS, VCTM, VAEA and VSAC conferences.
